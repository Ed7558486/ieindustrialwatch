Trade & supply chain

Thirty days to put your building in a foreign-trade zone. Your lease is what stops you.

A usage-driven foreign-trade zone site is approved in thirty days, and Riverside County and San Bernardino County are both inside an approved service area. The federal application runs on the tenant’s lease, and the standard Inland Empire use and alterations clauses do not carry it. The weekly-entry saving alone is $135,512 a year, or $0.90 per square foot, on a 150,000 square foot building.

Lease economics

If you signed in 2022, your renewal option is the most expensive way to stay

Inland Empire market asking rent jumped 25.3% in 2022 and has fallen every year since its 2023 peak. The rent floor in a standard renewal option now prices a 2022-vintage lease about 26% above market, roughly $4.3 million over a five-year renewal on 250,000 square feet.

Sep 11, 2026
Submarket spotlight

Your landlord's sale is a rent increase you never negotiated

Inland Empire industrial sales ran 8.7M SF in the first half, up 150% year over year. Each closing resets the property tax basis, and a NNN tenant pays it. For a 120,000 SF Jurupa Valley building that is about $158,970 a year, or $1.32 per square foot.
