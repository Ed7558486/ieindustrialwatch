English

I have worked in legal affairs for five U.S. subsidiaries of a Taiwanese public company for more than 11 years, and through that work I have known Edward L. Smith for just as long.

Our group has leased at least ten facilities in and around Cerritos, California, including offices, warehouses and light manufacturing plants in Cerritos, Norwalk, La Mirada, Santa Fe Springs, La Palma and elsewhere. Ed has also handled our leases out of state, in the Chicago and Miami areas. These facilities range from 50,000 to 150,000 square feet, and without exception we have relied on Ed to secure them on the most favorable terms.

Ed is honest, sincere, dedicated and easy to work with. His wife is Japanese American, and he has a real understanding of Asian culture, which makes him easy for Asian Americans to work with. He even knows a bit about feng shui.

If you need a real estate or land broker, Edward L. Smith is the only one I would recommend without reservation. I encourage you to give him every consideration.

— K. Kenneth Chang, Legal Affairs, Nien Made Group (2006)
